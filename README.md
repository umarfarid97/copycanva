# CanvaCopy — Modern Filipiniana Wedding Website

A recreation of a modern Filipiniana-style wedding website in beige and brown aesthetic, based on Canva design.

## Features
- **Authentic Aesthetics & Palette**: Filipiniana terracotta (`#bd8562`), warm cream (`#f7e7cd`), soft white (`#fff6e6`), and heritage brown (`#6f3f01`).
- **All 8 Sections Recreated 1:1**:
  - Hero / Home with couple title (*JaVi & BeA*), date/time, and botanical line art
  - Monogram & Featured Photo with overlay
  - Our Story (*Ang Simula*) with arched portrait frame
  - Reception Details (*Lugar ng Pagdarausan* & *Kasuotan*)
  - Schedule (*Programa*) with timeline items and dividers
  - Gift Registry with Sining Spaces contact card
  - FAQs (*Mga Katanungan*)
  - Contact & RSVP Footer with hashtag (*#JaviFoundBea*)
- **Interactive RSVP Modal Dialog**: Complete guest RSVP form with attendance, guest count, and notes.
- **Responsive Layout**: Adapts to desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.

## Quick Start
Open `index.html` directly in your browser or run:
```bash
npx serve .
```
